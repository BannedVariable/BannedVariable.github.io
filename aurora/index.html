    Download

    Get Aurora.

    Always the newest build, for your phone or computer.

    Android

    1. Download Aurora.apk on your phone.
    2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
    3. Tap Install. Updates install over it and keep your music.
    Download APK

    iPhone

    1. Install SideStore once (needs a computer one time).
    2. Tap Add to SideStore. Aurora installs and keeps itself updated.
    Add to SideStore Download .ipa

    No SideStore? Install the .ipa with Sideloadly from your computer. iPhones only run signed apps, so one of these is needed.

    Windows

    1. Download Aurora.exe. It's portable, no installer.
    2. Run it. If SmartScreen appears: More info → Run anyway.
    Download for Windows

    Or install it with a desktop shortcut from PowerShell:

    $ProgressPreference='SilentlyContinue'; $p="$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\Aurora"; ni -ItemType Directory -Force $p | Out-Null; iwr https://github.com/BannedVariable/aurora/releases/download/windows-latest/Aurora.exe -OutFile "$p\Aurora.exe"; Unblock-File "$p\Aurora.exe"; $s=(New-Object -ComObject WScript.Shell).CreateShortcut("$([Environment]::GetFolderPath('Desktop'))\Aurora.lnk"); $s.TargetPath="$p\Aurora.exe"; $s.IconLocation="$p\Aurora.exe,0"; $s.Save()

    Questions? Answers.

    Is Aurora really free?

    Yes. No ads, no subscription. The account is free too: it keeps your playlists, favorites and settings on every phone and computer you sign in on.

    Do I need an account?

    Yes, a free one. It is how Aurora knows which library is yours, so signing in on a second device brings your songs with you and both devices appear in the player, ready to hand playback over.

    Is Aurora connected to YouTube, SoundCloud or Spotify?

    No. Aurora is an independent, non-commercial player and hosts no music; it plays publicly available sources on your own device. Use it for private listening and only download music you have the right to.

    Why can't I just tap "install" on iPhone?

    iOS only runs apps signed with a certificate from Apple. Aurora doesn't use leaked certificates (those get revoked and can push anything to your phone), so on iPhone it installs through SideStore or Sideloadly with your own Apple ID.

    Why does my iPhone app stop opening after 7 days?

    Apps signed with a free Apple ID expire after 7 days. SideStore refreshes them automatically on your phone; with Sideloadly, just install again.

    Does music keep playing with the screen off?

    Yes, on iPhone and Android, with play, pause and skip on the lock screen.

    Where are my downloaded songs?

    Inside Aurora (Library → Offline). On iPhone they're also in the Files app under Aurora. On Android, downloads are also saved to Music/Aurora.
